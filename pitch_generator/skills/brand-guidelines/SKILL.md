## Palette
Primary: deep indigo, slate
Accent: warm amber, terracotta

## Light
Warm raking golden-hour light with long soft shadows. Single directional source creating high-contrast texture.

## Composition
Off-center hero subject following the rule of thirds, generous negative space, shallow depth of field with cinematic background blur.

## Subject
One realistic photographic subject crafted from authentic, handcrafted materials. Grounded, tangible textures with fine human details.

## Never
No neon, fluorescent colors, cyan/magenta duo-tones, watermarks, logos, ring light, or 3D renders.
